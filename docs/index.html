    How the signals work

    EMA 9/21 cross — the main system

    The 9 EMA crosses above the 21 EMA while Supertrend (10,3) is green, price is above a rising 50 EMA and RSI is 55 or more. Buy at the next open. The stop is the lowest low of the last 5 sessions; skip the trade if that is more than 12% away. Hold until Supertrend turns red, then sell at the next open.

    52-week-high base breakout

    The first new 52-week high after at least 6 months without one, with Supertrend green. Stop is the 5-day low, never more than 12% away. Same Supertrend exit.

    Sizing

    Risk 1% of capital per trade, at most 10 positions, each up to 20% of capital.

    Monthly all-time-high breakout

    On the monthly chart, find the month with the highest close ever. Buy when price trades above that month's high, if that month is at least 2 years old and the stock is above its 30-week average. Sell at the next open after a weekly close below the 30-week average. 10 stocks, equal weight. Backtest 2017–2026, all NSE stocks including delisted: 26.9% a year, worst fall −30%.

    Top Bottom

    Bank Nifty futures: buy above the last top, sell below the last bottom, always in the market, with a 1% stop on the entry day. Paper trading since 19 Oct 2026.

    OI alerts

    Trapped put writers: the biggest put open interest sits above the price. Trapped call writers: the biggest call open interest sits below it. In backtests single stocks did not fall after put traps; a market-wide share of 60% or more was mildly bearish.
