    How the signals work

    EMA 9/21 cross — the main system

    The 9 EMA crosses above the 21 EMA while Supertrend (10,3) is green, price is above a rising 50 EMA and RSI is 55 or more. Buy at the next open. The stop is the lowest low of the last 5 sessions; skip the trade if that is more than 12% away. Hold until Supertrend turns red, then sell at the next open.

    52-week-high base breakout

    The first new 52-week high after at least 6 months without one, with Supertrend green. Stop is the 5-day low, never more than 12% away. Same Supertrend exit.
